\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Typed decision models.}
TypeSafe's Jev~\cite{typesafejev} introduced the typed interface this paper
adopts, served through OpenRouter's Decisions API~\cite{openrouterdecisions},
and the design notes on building a coding agent around it~\cite{jevnote} argue
for the harness structure of Section~\ref{sec:programs}: explicit typed state,
context assembled per query rather than accumulated, routing priced by the
cost of rebuilding context rather than by tokens, and permissions as
decisions. Laya~\cite{laya} is an open, non-autoregressive implementation of
the same interface on encoder models, trained with rewards from strictly
proper scoring rules~\cite{gneiting2007}; Kai's checkpoints are Laya's. What
this paper adds is not a model but the frame around one: a fixed confidence
convention, programs bound to calibrations, the precedence of policy with its
consequences for promotion, and the extension to studies.

\paragraph{Encoders as classifiers.}
Scoring options against a text with a bidirectional encoder is the
cross-encoder of passage re-ranking~\cite{nogueira2019}, and treating a label
description as a hypothesis the text may entail is the entailment approach to
zero-shot classification~\cite{yin2019}; both descend from
BERT~\cite{devlin2019}. ModernBERT~\cite{modernbert} and mmBERT~\cite{mmbert}
are the encoders under Kai.

\paragraph{Structured output from generative models.}
Constrained decoding makes a generative model emit text that parses against a
grammar or schema~\cite{willard2023}. It removes parsing failures but not the
generation, and the probability it can report is that of a token sequence, not
a distribution over the caller's options. Tool-using agents~\cite{yao2023react,
schick2023toolformer} make decisions of the kind in Table~\ref{tab:programs}
inside the generative loop.

\paragraph{Guard models.}
Safety classifiers such as Llama Guard~\cite{inan2023llamaguard} judge prompts
and responses against a taxonomy of harms. A command-risk program is a guard
over actions rather than text, and Section~\ref{sec:authority} places any such
guard below a deterministic policy rather than in place of one.

\paragraph{Calibration and abstention.}
Temperature scaling~\cite{guo2017} is how the checkpoints' temperatures were
fitted, and the reject option~\cite{chow1970} and selective
classification~\cite{geifman2017} are the theory behind abstaining to \ask{}
below a threshold.

\paragraph{Authority as an order.}
Enforcing the join of two verdicts is the lattice model of secure information
flow~\cite{denning1976} applied to decisions, and a policy evaluated on every
action before it runs is a reference monitor~\cite{anderson1972}.

\paragraph{Decision analysis.}
The study model of Section~\ref{sec:studies} is additive multi-attribute
utility~\cite{keeney1976}. Sensitivity of multi-criteria rankings to their
weights is studied directly by Triantaphyllou and
S\'anchez~\cite{triantaphyllou1997}, and global sensitivity analysis more
broadly by Saltelli et al.~\cite{saltelli2008}; ranking cells by the shift
that flips a recommendation relative to their uncertainty is in the spirit of
the value of information~\cite{howard1966}. The two-system framing of fast
classification beside slow generation borrows Kahneman's
vocabulary~\cite{kahneman2011} and nothing more.
