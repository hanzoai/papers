\section{Limits}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{What is not measured.}
Each \ph{} is a number no kept run has produced: a Kai revision on
the harness (\texttt{bench preds}, then \texttt{merge.py}), native Laya
parity, each capability suite for each backend, speed included, and the whole
Enso evaluation, which is a design until its runs exist. Until they land, this paper claims no
accuracy for Kai.

\paragraph{What is not built.}
The evidence adapters, the 8K-context encoder, caching across calls, the
masked-slot decoder with its lattice, selector and distillation, refinement
inside the program executor, the Rust port of Laya's joint head, the
\op{/v1/decisions} service, the joint weight-change bound and sampled flip
probabilities are \proposed{} until they land on the main branch. Today's refinement writes
parents' answers into a question's state as text, a weaker conditional than a
trained slot model. The media rows compare Kai with baselines reading text
renderings, which measures the renderings as much as the models.

\paragraph{What is not won.}
Off its training mixture Laya trails Jev: on Banking77 (\res{kj/banking77full/laya/accuracy} against
\res{kj/banking77full/jev/accuracy}), on jailbreak detection
(\res{kj/guardrailsjailbreak/laya/accuracy} against
\res{kj/guardrailsjailbreak/jev/accuracy}), and on MASSIVE outside English
(\res{kj/massive/laya/nonenglishmacro} against
\res{kj/massive/jev/nonenglishmacro} macro). Its router sends typed-decision
states to the English checkpoint, which scores
\res{kj/typeddecisions/laya/accuracy}. Kai trains on the train splits of every
harness source, so its leads over a model that did not are in-distribution;
with Jev's training data unknown, no table here measures generalisation to a
new task.

\paragraph{Known failure modes of Laya.}
Its authors report that boolean-word labels can be followed instead of their
descriptions, negated requests answered as unnegated, \op{noul} answers driven
by the labels rather than the state, and a score's first level rarely picked
by the multilingual checkpoint~\cite{laya} \reported. A program's tests should
add a negated and a relabelled variant of each case.

\paragraph{Calibration is assumed, then checked.}
Thresholds, abstention, conformal sets and flip probabilities take
probabilities at their word; a shift in the evidence distribution breaks fitted
temperatures and pinned calibrations alike, and only shadow records detect
it.

\paragraph{Adversarial state beyond the lattice.}
Proposition~\ref{prop:tighten} protects constraints, not preferences: an
adversary can push routing toward a cheaper model or completion toward an early
finish. Those need deterministic backstops, such as requiring a passing test
run in the transcript. Deliberate friction is bounded by policy, not
prevented.

\paragraph{The study model has assumptions.}
Additive scores need preferentially independent criteria; one-parameter flips
hold the rest fixed; assumption sweeps are a grid; sampled flip probabilities
treat cells as independent, which fails when two read the same evidence.
