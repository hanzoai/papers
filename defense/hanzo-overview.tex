\vspace{7pt}

\textbf{Hanzo Cloud is the sovereign AI operating layer for the new American industrial base} --- factories,
robots, vehicles, sensors, drones, ships, and defense systems. We combine open-source AI infrastructure, spatial
3D, robotics control, post-quantum cryptography, FHE, and low-latency on-chain coordination so industrial and
defense operators can \textbf{build, automate, simulate, secure, and command} real-world systems --- from the
factory floor to the contested edge --- without depending on closed, foreign, or hyperscaler-controlled AI.

\vspace{3pt}\rulen\vspace{2pt}

{\large\textbf{Why Hanzo}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Sovereign industrial cloud.} An open-source, self-hostable AI cloud --- agents, models, tools,
  memory, IAM/KMS, audit, sandboxes, deployment, secure workflows --- running in cloud, factory, edge, or
  air-gapped. Not just chatbot infrastructure.
  \item \textbf{Spatial 3D + robotics.} Sits behind robotic cells, digital twins, spatial maps, autonomous
  vehicles, drones, inspection, and simulation-to-field workflows --- exactly the Michigan industrial base of
  vehicles, autonomy, robotics, and ground systems.
  \item \textbf{Low-latency on-chain coordination.} Verifiable coordination \emph{for machines}: identity,
  provenance, command logs, audit trails, access control, encrypted state, settlement, and trusted multi-party
  workflows between humans, robots, contractors, factories, and battlefield nodes.
  \item \textbf{Post-quantum + FHE-native security.} PQC secures comms and identity against future
  cryptographic risk; FHE lets agents, rules, and workflows compute \emph{over ciphertext} --- coalition
  analytics, cross-domain decisions, private supply-chain scoring, encrypted sensor fusion, private robotics
  telemetry.
  \item \textbf{Agentic engineering + cost collapse.} The same stack modernizes a contractor's software in
  place --- fewer services, lower cloud cost, sovereign and auditable --- as proven below.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{2pt}\rulen\vspace{2pt}

{\large\textbf{Built for physical industry}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Factory automation} --- agentic workflows for production, QA, maintenance, scheduling, inventory, and process optimization.
  \item \textbf{Robotics \& autonomy} --- spatial 3D, simulation, perception pipelines, low-latency control, fleet coordination, and edge inference.
  \item \textbf{Defense systems} --- secure command \& telemetry, post-quantum identity, FHE-protected workflows, DDIL operation, and air-gapped deployment.
  \item \textbf{Industrial software modernization} --- collapse legacy cloud, services, CI/CD, observability, auth, data, and security into an owned OSS stack.
  \item \textbf{On-chain machine coordination} --- tamper-evident logs, provenance, access control, encrypted state, tasking, settlement, and inter-org trust.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{2pt}\rulen\vspace{2pt}

{\large\textbf{The proof}}\quad Founding CEO/CTO \textbf{Zach Kelling} was the \textbf{\#1 Anthropic user by
token count worldwide in 2025} (claudecount.com). That throughput built --- with a small team on the owned OSS
stack --- a \textbf{FINRA/SEC-approved, SOC\,2 securities exchange} on post-quantum crypto, a native-GPU
blockchain, and FHE. Engaged for a narrow remediation scope, the team uncovered the real estate ---
\textbf{200+ microservices} on Google Cloud --- and \textbf{consolidated it into 3 binaries}, deleting
\textbf{3.5M lines of dead code} (3.07M${\rightarrow}$1.06M active) and closing \textbf{93 security findings}
(22 critical). Cloud spend fell \textbf{${\sim}90\%$} (${\sim}\$35\text{K}\!\rightarrow\!{\sim}\$3.5\text{K}$/mo,
${\approx}\$380\text{K}$/yr saved). Backed by production post-quantum (FIPS 203/204/205), 50 machine-checked
proofs, and the ZAP transport at \textbf{11.5M tx/s} (91\% less memory, 96\% less energy than JSON-RPC).
